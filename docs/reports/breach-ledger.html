Breach Ledger

The landmark website compromises, data breaches, worms and cyberattacks since the Morris worm, one row per incident, reduced to what went wrong, who did it, how it came to light, and how it ended.

No list of “every” breach exists: regulators log tens of thousands a year, most never made public. This register holds the incidents that set records, changed laws, took down national systems, or defined a country's cyber history. Figures are as reported at the time; attribution is as stated by governments, courts or the victim, and “claimed” means only the attacker said so. Records through early October 2026.

Sources are the victims' own disclosures, court filings, regulator decisions (FTC, ICO, CNIL, OAIC, PDPC and others), CERT advisories and the reporting of Krebs on Security, BleepingComputer, Reuters, the BBC and national press. Where accounts conflict, the court or regulator figure is used.
